# Data and publication boundary

KuaiRec by Chongming Gao and collaborators, CIKM 2022; source https://kuairec.com/ and https://zenodo.org/records/18164998 (DOI 10.5281/zenodo.18164998). Interactions are historical 2020 feedback; January 2026 is a packaging date.

Zenodo metadata says CC BY 4.0 while the archive and repository contain CC BY-SA 4.0. The conflict is unresolved. Preserve the embedded notice; conservatively treat dataset-derived aggregate reports, tables and figures as CC BY-SA 4.0 with attribution and an indication of modifications. This does not settle whether trained assets are licensed adaptations. Original project code has a separate MIT license.

Local research only. Public-preparation inventory excludes raw/transformed interactions, real user/item maps, per-user metrics, candidate lists, probabilities/scores, embeddings and checkpoints. Use official acquisition instructions. No publication has been performed. No publisher endorsement is implied.

We derive completion labels, bounded playback rewards, static pair holdouts, and explicitly synthetic observation/candidate experiments. Missing cells are not dislikes; completion is not satisfaction; known synthetic probabilities are not historical exposure probabilities. The large matrix is audited, never used for training.
